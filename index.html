FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often should an Ancaster home have the ducts cleaned?

Every two to three years covers most homes here. Wooded lots push the short end of that with leaf mould and pollen, and homes with pets or recent renovations belong there too. Pop a return cover and look: that answer is usually obvious.

Are you certified and insured?

Fully certified and insured, with documentation available on request. Ontario does not regulate duct cleaning as a trade, so those credentials, plus training and references, are exactly how you should be vetting us, and we encourage it.

How will I know the work was really done?

Photographs of your own trunk lines, before and after, shown before we leave the driveway. Ancaster customers read contracts closely, and we would not have it any other way.

Our dryer has been slow since we moved in. Vent or machine?

In larger homes with long runs, the vent is guilty far more often than the dryer. We measure flow at the hood and tell you which one is failing, and if it is the machine we say that instead of selling a cleaning it does not need.

What happens during a visit, and how long?

Truck vacuum connected, registers washed, each branch brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos taken. Ancaster homes run three to five hours depending on size and how many returns a builder decided to install.

Fall start-up smells musty. Can you fix that?

Yes. The odour is residue on the duct walls from a damp idle season and whatever the system moved last year. Cleaning lifts it, then a sanitizer treatment on the clean surfaces handles what is left.

We clean constantly and the house stays dusty. Why?

Because the duct system is likely leaking on the return side, drawing air from the mechanical room and wall cavities and distributing it house-wide. We can test for that and seal the joints it finds.

Do you work outside Ancaster proper?

Yes. We cover the whole community plus Dundas, Hamilton, Waterdown, Copetown, Carlisle, and Freelton, essentially everything inside the Hamilton border and just beyond it.

Do you serve areas outside of Ancaster, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Ancaster and nearby communities including Hamilton, Dundas, Waterdown, Copetown, and the surrounding area.
